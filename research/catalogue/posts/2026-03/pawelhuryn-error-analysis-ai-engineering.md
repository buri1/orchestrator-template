## Key Takeaways

1. **85% of AI initiatives fail — error analysis beats metrics** — The core claim: successful teams start by understanding *why* things fail, not by optimizing metrics. This is a "measure what matters" argument applied to AI engineering. Metrics without error taxonomy lead to vanity optimization.
2. **Error-first methodology for agent systems** — For agent orchestration, this translates to: before adding more agents or features, catalog the failure modes of existing agents. What causes agents to get stuck? What produces bad code? What triggers infinite loops? The answers compound faster than feature additions.
3. **Aligns with our Principle 7 (build what you needed)** — Error analysis is inherently backward-looking and practical. It prevents speculative engineering by focusing energy on observed failures rather than theoretical improvements.
