## Key Takeaways

1. **The web itself is the biggest threat to AI agents** — Not sophisticated attackers, but the inherent structure of the web (misleading links, CAPTCHAs, dark patterns, honeypots, consent traps) creates systematic failure modes for autonomous agents. This reframes agent security from "defense against attackers" to "resilience against the environment."
2. **First systematic framework for agent traps** — The DeepMind paper provides a taxonomy and classification system for web-based traps that can ensnare autonomous agents. Having a framework means we can design defensive patterns rather than playing whack-a-mole.
3. **High signal from omarsar0 curation** — 458 likes and 38K views on a research paper share indicates strong practitioner interest in agent safety/robustness, not just capabilities.
