## What Is This

A YouTube video covering two complementary project planning frameworks used in program evaluation, social impact work, and grant-funded projects. A **Logic Model** is a compact linear diagram mapping inputs to activities to outputs to outcomes -- it answers "what" will happen and is primarily a compliance/reporting tool. A **Theory of Change** goes deeper, explaining "why" change will occur by documenting the causal mechanisms and assumptions connecting activities to outcomes. The Theory of Change is ideally developed before the program is designed (to determine the best intervention), while the Logic Model is constructed afterward to describe and track the program. Together they form a roadmap (ToC) with a step-by-step guide (Logic Model).
