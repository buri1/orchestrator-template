## What Is This

Bilawal Sidhu, a former Google Senior PM (6 years on Maps, ARCore Geospatial API, Immersive View, and 3D mapping at global scale), built "WorldView" -- a personal geospatial command center that fuses open-source intelligence (OSINT) feeds onto a navigable 3D globe. When the Iran strikes (Operation Epic Fury) began, Sidhu deployed an AI agent swarm to record every publicly available signal before caches cleared: satellite surveillance passes, GPS jamming zones, commercial and military flight tracks, maritime AIS data at the Strait of Hormuz, and airspace closures across nine countries. The result was a full 4D (3D + time) minute-by-minute reconstruction of the operation. The project went viral, with viewers expressing concern for Sidhu's safety given the sensitivity of the intelligence reconstruction. WorldView was set to open to the public in April 2026.
