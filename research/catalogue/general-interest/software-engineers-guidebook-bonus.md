## Key Takeaways

1. **Career Stagnation Patterns** — Recognizing when senior engineers feel "stuck" and actionable solutions: clarifying expectations with management, focusing on high-impact work over busywork, and seeking lateral growth opportunities. Relevant for anyone managing engineering teams or self-managing a solo practice.

2. **System Migration Playbook** — Monitoring strategies, pre-mortem planning, staged rollouts, and data migration considerations. This is the playbook for any infrastructure change — including migrating from one orchestration approach to another.

3. **Equitable Career Growth** — Addresses biases in performance evaluations and promotion criteria for senior engineers. Important framing for how AI tools change what "high-performing engineer" means.

4. **Product Mindset for Engineers** — Aligning technical work with business outcomes and understanding organizational profit structures. The key question: does your technical work generate revenue, reduce cost, or mitigate risk? If you can't answer, you're building the wrong thing.

5. **Technology Selection at Scale** — Balancing autonomy with governance in rapidly growing startups. When to let teams choose their own stack vs. when to enforce standards. Directly relevant to our multi-project orchestrator where different client projects use different tech stacks.

## Summary

This is a companion document to Gergely Orosz's "The Software Engineer's Guidebook" — ten additional chapters totaling approximately 100 pages, freely accessible on Scribd. The bonus chapters extend the main book's coverage into areas like career stagnation (recognizing and escaping plateau patterns), system migrations (monitoring, pre-mortems, staged rollouts), equitable career growth (addressing evaluation biases), product mindset development (aligning technical work with business outcomes), product-engineering team dynamics, technology selection governance, and capacity planning during migrations.

Gergely Orosz is the author of The Pragmatic Engineer Newsletter (#1 technology newsletter on Substack with 500,000+ readers) and brings a decade of engineering experience at JP Morgan, Skype/Microsoft, Skyscanner, and Uber. The bonus chapters maintain the same practical, experience-driven tone as the main guidebook.

While not directly related to agent orchestration, the system migration playbook and product mindset frameworks are useful reference material for anyone building and selling engineering automation systems.
