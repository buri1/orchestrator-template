## Summary

The article reviews **Graphite** (graphite.rs), a browser-based, fully open-source 2D design application. The author, a tech writer at MakeUseOf, expected another shallow web-based Photoshop imitation but found it to be a substantively capable design tool accessible from any machine through a browser.

The key differentiator highlighted is the node-based editing interface (node editor) which provides non-destructive, procedural design capabilities — a more advanced workflow paradigm than typical clone tools. The author frames the open-source/free nature as almost incidental; the real appeal is the functional quality.

Compared to Adobe Creative Suite alternatives, Graphite's browser-native approach eliminates installation friction and platform lock-in. The article targets general consumers looking for cost-effective creative software, not developers or AI practitioners.
