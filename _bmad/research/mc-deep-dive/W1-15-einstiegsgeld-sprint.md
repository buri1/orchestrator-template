## 1. SITUATION ASSESSMENT — What We Actually Know

### 1.1 The Einstiegsgeld Program (Facts from Documents)

The Einstiegsgeld is a German Jobcenter subsidy for people transitioning from unemployment to self-employment. It provides monthly financial support (typically 50-75% of ALG I equivalent) for 6-12 months while the business gets established.

**What Burak submitted** (from `project_einstiegsgeld_deadline_50_tage.md`):
- A Kooperationsplan (cooperation plan) with milestones
- The plan promises "erste Verträge mit Kunden" (first contracts with customers) by the end of the subsidy period
- The hard milestone date is **2026-05-31**
- There is a Mitwirkungsschreiben dated 16.12.2025 with a deadline of 02.01.2026 — status UNKNOWN, potentially 2K+ EUR at risk

**What the Jobcenter actually evaluates** (based on standard Einstiegsgeld review process):
- They want to see **plausible business activity**, not necessarily profitability
- "Erste Verträge" means signed agreements or invoices — not necessarily paid revenue
- They look for: business registration (Gewerbeanmeldung), tax registration, evidence of customer acquisition efforts, and ideally at least one signed contract or invoice
- A Rechnung (invoice) counts. A Kostenvoranschlag (quote) counts less. An LOI or Angebot counts if co-signed.
- They do NOT typically demand profitability at this stage — they demand evidence of genuine business activity and forward momentum
